POP APART!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Pop Apart! (the "Game") is a puzzle game developed and published by Tan Truong ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

In short: The Game has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. To improve the Game we collect anonymous gameplay statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). When you play, the Game sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. These include:

  • Game events, such as starting, completing, failing or leaving a level; using a hint; finishing the tutorial; seeing a new mechanic; and changing music, sound or vibration settings.
  • Values attached to those events, such as the level number, time spent in the level, number of blocked moves, hints used, stars earned, how far the model was rotated, and the highest level you have completed.
  • Information collected automatically by Firebase: an app instance identifier (a random ID created when the Game is first opened, not linked to your identity), device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate country or region (derived by Google from your IP address), first-open time and session information.

We do not request permission to track you, and the Game does not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS).

Information stored only on your device. The Game saves your progress (levels completed, stars), your settings (music, sound, vibration) and a local gameplay log used for testing and improving levels. This information stays on your device. The Game does not send it to us; it is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. How we use information

  • To understand how players progress through levels, so we can balance difficulty and improve the tutorial.
  • To find problems in the Game and fix them.
  • To measure how many people play and keep playing.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

3. Service providers

Gameplay statistics are processed by Google LLC as our service provider for Google Analytics for Firebase. Google may process this data on servers outside your country. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Data retention

Gameplay statistics are kept in Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months and then deleted automatically. Data stored on your device is kept until you delete the Game.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all locally stored data and resets the app instance identifier.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask what data we hold, or to ask us to delete it. Because statistics are anonymous, we may ask for details that help us find them (for example, your device model and roughly when you played).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for gameplay statistics is our legitimate interest in improving the Game.
  • If you are a California resident, you have the right to know about and request deletion of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We rely on Google's security measures for data processed by Firebase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information described in this policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing or release notes.
